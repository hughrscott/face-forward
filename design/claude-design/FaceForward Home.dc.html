THE POSITION
Backing in is planning your exit. We plan to arrive.
Every day, millions of drivers reverse into parking spots as if the supermarket were a getaway. They arrive braced to flee. Three-point turns. Backup cameras. Doubt. We ask, respectfully: what are you running from?
FaceForward is a movement for people who arrive on purpose. Nose in. Head up. Bumper to the line. We don't park to leave. We park to be somewhere.
THE THREE-POINT PLEDGE
One motion. Zero shame.
01 — APPROACH
See the spot. Want the spot. Do not circle the lot negotiating with yourself.
02 — COMMIT
One turn of the wheel. No shimmying. Your ancestors crossed oceans facing forward.
03 — ARRIVE
Front bumper to the line. Engine off. You are here now. Be here.
